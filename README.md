# Degrees of Lewdity: X-Change Edition

> You take a pill. And you turn into an incredibly attractive opposite-sex version of yourself (try to keep control of what happens next!).

DoLX is a mod build of Degrees of Lewdity that **adds the X-Change pill system, DoLPlus integration, extra events and content, gender identity mechanics, encounter/sex additions, quality-of-life changes, audio support, and more!**

The idea is simple: X-Change is a fictional brand of sex-swapping pills. In this mod, they are part of the world instead of just being a one-time gimmick. You can buy the pills, have them forced on you, find them used during events, or see NPCs use them. They can also affect routes, encounters, and how other characters treat you. Because of this, some scenes can play out differently from Vanilla DoL or DoL Plus.

**Join DoL:X today!**
And start enjoying both sexes!
For spoilers, routes, and more specific mechanics, see the **[Wiki](https://dolmodding.miraheze.org/wiki/DoL_Plus/X-Change)**.
